Frequently asked

Everything you want to know
before diving in.

Can't find your answer? Send us your question.

How much does an inground pool cost in Montréal?

Pricing ranges from CA$55,000 to CA$130,000 depending on size, finish (concrete, fiberglass or liner) and landscaping. Aqua Élite delivers a free, detailed quote within 48 hours.

What is the typical timeline for a pool installation?

Plan 8 to 14 weeks from contract signing to first dive, including municipal permits. Construction starts as soon as the ground thaws (mid-April).

Do I need a permit to install a pool in Quebec?

Yes. A municipal permit is mandatory and an enclosure compliant with Quebec's Residential Swimming Pool Safety Regulation is required. Aqua Élite handles all paperwork.

Do you offer pool opening and winterization?

Yes. Spring opening from CA$295 and full winterization from CA$345, including water analysis, treatment and safety cover handling.

What warranty do you offer?

10-year structural warranty on the shell, 5 years on equipment (pump, filter, heater) and lifetime workmanship warranty on Aqua Élite installations.
